# Plan: SEC EDGAR source and capex marts (Phase 2 source, prepares analysis (b))

Status: PLANNED · 2026-10-06

## Goal

1. Add source `sec_edgar`: XBRL `companyfacts` for a configured list of companies (PLAN §2.1 #11),
   **Exact** (facts carry their filing accession and `filed` date; restatements are new facts).
2. Add mart `030_capex`: quarterly capital expenditure per company and per company group, as of
   any date, derived from the cash-flow statement facts.
3. Prepare starter analysis (b), "capex vs price collapse" (EDGAR capex × analysis (a)), **without
   building it**. Done when the mart gives hyperscaler capex per calendar quarter as of a date,
   with completeness flags, on synthetic fixtures in CI and on real data locally.

Out of scope: the analysis (b) exploration; segment facts (Azure, Data Center revenue: PLAN §2.1
scope limit, curated disclosures); `frames`/`companyconcept` APIs; revenue and other measures
(the stage keeps every fact, so a later mart can add them without a re-fetch); new dependencies
(none needed).

## What was measured (2026-10-06)

- `https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK##########.json`, served gzip when asked:
  43–335 KB per company, 158k facts for the 7 companies below. Each fact: `start` (duration facts
  only), `end`, `val`, `accn`, `fy`, `fp`, `form`, `filed`, optional `frame`. `cik` is an int for
  some companies and a zero-padded string for others.
- **Terms.** sec.gov: "Information presented on sec.gov is considered public information and may
  be copied or further distributed … Please consider appropriate citation to the SEC as the
  source." Fair access: ≤ 10 requests/second, declared User-Agent with contact.
- **Capex tags.** All seven use `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment` except Amazon
  (`PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets` from FY2016 comparatives on) and NVIDIA (the same, from
  FY2020). Amazon's two tags overlap on FY2016 with different meanings (old tag 6.74 bn, net of
  proceeds and incentives; new tag 7.80 bn, gross).
- **Durations.** Cash-flow facts are year-to-date: 3/6/9/12 months from the fiscal-year start.
  Some companies also tag the discrete quarter (Microsoft: 102 three-month facts), others mostly
  do not. Q4 exists only as FY − 9M. Fiscal years end in June (Microsoft), May (Oracle), late
  January (NVIDIA, 52/53 weeks) or December.
- **Restatements.** The same period appears in several filings (comparatives). Values differ for
  Alphabet, Meta, NVIDIA and CoreWeave on some periods (restatements and rounding, e.g. Meta
  2021 Q1 4,272 vs 4,303 m). Old facts remain in the file, so selection by `filed` ≤ as-of is
  reproducible from a later fetch.
- **Finance leases.** Microsoft and Amazon describe capex "including finance leases"; the cash
  tag excludes them. `RightOfUseAssetObtainedInExchangeForFinanceLeaseLiability` (new finance-
  lease assets, non-cash) exists for all five hyperscalers.
- 8-K facts exist (earnings releases) and are excluded: only 10-K, 10-Q and amendments count.

## Design

### 1. Companies (`config/entities/tickers.yaml`, PLAN §3)

`cik`, `name`, `group` (`hyperscaler` | `neocloud` | `supplier`), optional `vendor` (id in
`vendors.yaml`). Initial list: MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, ORCL (hyperscaler), CRWV (neocloud), NVDA
(supplier). Keyed by ticker; CIK is the join key (tickers change, e.g. FB → META).

### 2. Fetch

- One request per company, `User-Agent: $SEC_EDGAR_USER_AGENT` (required secret), `Accept-Encoding:
  gzip`, ≥ 0.15 s between requests. Validate before storing: JSON object with `facts`, and its
  `cik` equals the requested one. Stored as served: `companyfacts_CIK##########.json[.gz]`.
- `vintage = {kind: edgar_filed, value: <newest accession>, date: <its filed date>, window:
  [<oldest filed>, <fetch date − 1>]}`. The window makes `build.cutoff` treat the source as Exact
  (selected by source time). Cadence weekly (filings are quarterly; a weekly fetch is at most a
  week late).

### 3. Stage

Reads, per CIK, **the newest fetch that contains it** (append-only: a later file holds every
earlier fact). Tables:
- `facts`: `cik (String, 10 digits), taxonomy, tag, unit, period_start (Date, null for instants),
  period_end, val (Float64), accn, fy (Int64), fp, form, filed (Date), frame, fetch_id`.
- `companies`: `cik, ticker, name, group, vendor_id, entity_name (SEC's), fetch_id, fetched_at`.
- `_rejected_rows`: facts with an unparsable date or value (expected 0).
- `vintage_at(as_of)`: newest accession with `filed` ≤ as_of across staged facts.

### 4. Mart `030_capex` (inputs: `sec_edgar`)

Measures and tag precedence (in the SQL, documented in SOURCE.md):
- `capex`: `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets` (1), `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment` (2).
- `finance_lease_additions`: `RightOfUseAssetObtainedInExchangeForFinanceLeaseLiability`.

Steps:
1. Facts: `us-gaap`, unit USD, duration facts, forms 10-K/10-Q(/A), `filed ≤ as_of`. Per (company,
   tag, start, end) keep the **latest filed** (restatements supersede earlier values as of when
   they were filed).
2. Duration in months = round(days / 30.44), kept when within 8 days of 3, 6, 9 or 12 months.
3. Quarters: a 3-month fact is a quarter (`reported`). A 6/9/12-month fact minus the fact with the
   same start and 3 months less gives the quarter `(prior end + 1, end)` (`ytd_difference`).
   A reported quarter wins over a derived one with the same end.
4. One tag per (company, measure, quarter): the highest precedence present. The tag is kept so
   a switch is visible.
5. Calendar quarter = the quarter containing the period's midpoint (Oracle Jun–Aug → Q3; NVIDIA
   May–Jul → Q2).

Tables:
- `edgar_quarterly`: one row per company × measure × fiscal quarter with `cal_quarter`, value,
  method, tag, accession, filed, ticker, group.
- `capex_quarterly`: per company × calendar quarter: `capex_usd`, `finance_lease_additions_usd`,
  `capex_incl_finance_leases_usd` (upper bound).
- `capex_group_quarterly`: per group × calendar quarter: sums, `companies_reported`,
  `companies_expected` (group companies whose first capex quarter is on or before it),
  `complete`. The latest quarter is usually incomplete (Oracle reports first); analyses must use
  complete quarters only or say so.

### 5. Tests (synthetic companyfacts only)

Fetch: User-Agent sent, gzip stored as served, CIK mismatch and non-JSON refused, missing secret
skips. Stage: newest fetch per CIK, cik normalised, instants have null start. Mart: YTD
differencing incl. Q4 = FY − 9M; reported quarter preferred; restatement selected by as-of;
8-K excluded; tag precedence and switch; 52/53-week durations; midpoint calendar quarter;
group completeness; `vintage_at`.

### 6. Caveats to carry into analysis (b)

- Cash paid for property and equipment, not capex "including finance leases" as Microsoft and
  Amazon present it; the finance-lease column is an upper bound (new leased assets, not cash).
- Company-wide capex, not AI-only: includes warehouses (Amazon), offices, devices. No segment
  split in v1.
- Fiscal quarters mapped to calendar quarters by midpoint; Oracle and NVIDIA are offset by one or
  two months.
- NVIDIA's tag includes intangible assets; NVIDIA is a supplier, not in the hyperscaler sum.
